My home setup splits duties across three spots: a compact 10-inch rack handling routing, switching, and PoE Wi-Fi; a tower running VMs, containers, and local storage; and a Mac Studio dedicated to local LLMs and development workloads.

It doubles as a safe testbed for breaking networking configs, self-hosting services, and offloading heavy coding-agent jobs.
